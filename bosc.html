Bosc

Children colour a sheet of paper; a phone photographs it, and the drawing comes alive on the big screen.

builtpublic beta at bosc.live

A sheet coloured with any pencils or markers, a phone, and a TV, projector or laptop. The creature on the screen keeps the child’s own colours.

How it works

printAn A4 colouring sheet from bosc.live. Four black squares in its corners let the phone find the page.
colourWith any pencils, crayons or markers. No printer? Colour straight on a phone or tablet, or start from a ready-made drawing.
snapScan the QR code on the big screen to join the room, and photograph the sheet. The phone straightens the page and cuts the drawing out; seconds later the creature is on the screen, moving.

Three worlds

forestdeer, fox, hare, hedgehog, owl, butterfly
oceanclownfish, dolphin, jellyfish, shark, turtle, whale
spacerocket, satellite, UFO, astronaut and two kinds of alien

Each drawing becomes an animated creature inside a 3D diorama — a meadow by a pond, the sea, the Moon with the Earth and planets overhead. The creatures play out small scenes on their own: a rocket launches, a satellite orbits, a UFO zips past, jellyfish pulse.

One room, any screen

A room is one shared big screen — a TV at home, a projector in a classroom, a laptop at a party — and everyone who scans its code adds their drawings to it. The big screen runs in any modern browser with WebGL 2; the phone needs no app. In English, Spanish, Catalan, French and Russian.

For parents and teachers

no accountsA room is an unguessable link: no sign-up, no password, no cookies, no analytics.
the photoIt is straightened and cropped on the phone; only the cut-out drawing is sent to the room.
drawingsKept with the room and deleted with it — automatically after 30 days without use, or at once with “Delete room”.
the serverKeeps no access logs and stores no IP addresses.
